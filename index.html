About

I am currently an undergraduate student in Mechatronic Engineering at South China University of Technology. I will join the University of Science and Technology of China as a master's student in Control Science and Engineering. Previously, I worked as a research intern at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interest is Robotics and Embodied AI, with a focus on dexterous manipulation. My research goal is to build generalizable and dexterous robot policies that can reliably operate in the open world.

Projects

Prior-GA: Few-Shot In-Context Imitation Learning via Prior-Guided Graph Alignment for Robotic Manipulation

Independent first author. Diagnosed attention dilution in in-context imitation learning and proposed prior-guided graph alignment: VLM-based Task-ROI extraction + FPFH/ICP registration. Single-demonstration RLBench success: 27.78% → 72.22% (+44.4pp).

In-Context IL RLBench +44.4pp VLM

π0.5 VLA Post-Training & Real-Robot Deployment

Full-stack pipeline from VR-based teleoperation data collection to real-robot deployment on the Piper arm. 14-DoF bimanual states + three RGB-D streams, π0.5 LoRA post-training with ROS2-based closed-loop execution. RoboTwin Pose-VLA evaluation: 85.8% across 50 tasks.

π0.5 · LoRA RoboTwin 85.8% Dual-Arm Teleop

第二届珠海国际灵巧操作挑战赛 · 第四名

Dual JAKA arms with a LinkerHand five-fingered dexterous hand for peg-in-hole and object sorting tasks under strict time constraints.

Dexterous Hand Dual-Arm System

第二十届全国大学生智能汽车竞赛 · 国家一等奖（全国季军）

Team leader. Designed an X-Z 4-DoF manipulator from scratch; PD control with visual end compensation cut alignment time from 20.7s to 1.5s. Jetson Orin Nano multi-process system, 100% completion rate across ~20 national-round runs.
