Introduction

I am currently an undergraduate student in Mechatronic Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT). I will join the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) as a master's student in Control Science and Engineering. Previously, I worked as a research intern at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interest is Robotics and Embodied AI, with a focus on dexterous manipulation. My research goal is to build generalizable and dexterous robot policies that can reliably operate in the open world — enabling robots to genuinely assist humanity in everyday tasks.
